- Outcome and acceptance criteria: a chart with estimated position, movement orders to a map coordinate with a deadline (miss = lose a resupply point and rations), a `map` module, and a separate Helm screen with compass and wheel; deterministic and replayable; the night watch unchanged
- In scope / out of scope: in = sim-core (MOVE messages, order tracking, dead-reckoning, events), desk Helm and chart, store, strings, tests, docs; out = autopilot, land or depth collision in the boat model, enemy positions on the chart
- Material assumptions and risks: orders are 10-25 km away because the grid's finest resolution is 10 km and the boat is slow; the assumed deadline speed (8 kn) and margin are balance numbers; CI not run (rate limited)

## Architecture linkage

Endless picks `MOVE` -> desk decodes and the player accepts -> `ORDER_ACCEPTED` -> sim checks the true position each step -> `ORDER_REACHED`/`ORDER_FAILED` -> store -> desk rations, Orders and Helm; the chart shows only `navFix()` (estimate).

## Progress and decisions

- Decision: the order's cell is a destination, so grid and bearing checks are skipped for it and its forgery pool is fist/timestamp only (D8 test over 300 seeds and all Intel levels).
- Decision: the estimate is the true position plus a seeded random-walk drift proportional to distance sailed, with a shown error radius; the arrival radius (5 km) is far larger than the drift on an ordered leg.
- Decision: steering widgets are hidden on the radio desk and live on the Helm; the existing Actions are reused, so no new sim command exists for steering.
- Found while testing: my first UI script accepted the newest message instead of the one it had decoded (selection moved when a message arrived); the app was right, the script fixed.
